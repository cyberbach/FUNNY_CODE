About Winbush
Jonathan Winbush is a multi–award-winning motion graphics artist who has worked professionally since 2006. His credits span blockbuster films and major franchises, including Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer, Iron Man, Transformers, My Little Pony, LEGO Harry Potter, and Power Rangers. Since discovering Unreal Engine in 2019, Winbush has become a leading voice in bringing real-time technology to motion design, pioneering workflows that empower artists to create faster, more dynamic, and uncompromised visuals.
