# Shawnthebro

Hello, all! I am Shawnthebro! I love making content for others to enjoy! I am a software engineer that worked on Bad Manors (Lead Programmer) and Fury Strike: 80s Beatdown (Core Software Engineer). Bad Manors is a free game on Kongregate that was made in Unity with C#. Bad Manors was made as a college project using Castlevania for inspiration. Fury Strike: 80s Beatdown is a free game on Steam that was made in Unreal Engine 4 with C++. Fury Strike was made in collaboration with Full Sail University and Rooster Teeth (and shown off at RTX) using Street Fighter and Tekken for inspiration. Outside of programming, I am also a Twitch streamer who loves to play games: https://www.twitch.tv/shawnthebro27.
All of my video descriptions use my "reborn" format. This means that any videos I uploaded before January 21st, 2019 will have the description formatting from later videos. I am not claiming that the Skyrim Theme or Star Theme were created for my channel.
